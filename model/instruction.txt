## Download the model

This app runs Llama 2 7B Chat, 4-bit GGML (q4_0), through CTransformers on CPU.

File: llama-2-7b-chat.ggmlv3.q4_0.bin (~3.8 GB)
From: https://huggingface.co/TheBloke/Llama-2-7B-Chat-GGML/blob/main/llama-2-7b-chat.ggmlv3.q4_0.bin

Put it in this folder:

    model/llama-2-7b-chat.ggmlv3.q4_0.bin

Or point MODEL_PATH in .env at wherever you keep it.
